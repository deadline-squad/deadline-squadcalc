# Tests

`npm test` runs `node --test` over the `tests/*.test.mjs` glob (no extra dependency; Node >= 22.13; from Node 22 a directory
argument is no longer searched, it fails with `ERR_UNSUPPORTED_DIR_IMPORT`, so the script names the files by glob; CI runs it on
Node 22). `leaflet.test.mjs`, `groundShapes.test.mjs`, `protectionZone.test.mjs` and `structureInfo.test.mjs` import the packages
`leaflet` and `i18next`: run `npm ci` first, on a checkout without `node_modules` they stop with `ERR_MODULE_NOT_FOUND`. The pure modules
(`squadBallistics.js`, `squadDamage.js`, `squadHeightmaps.js`, `squadFiringSolution.js`, `squadWeapons.js`,
`weapons.js`) import `../app.js` (the whole UI); `register.mjs` preloads `loader.mjs`, which replaces it
with `stubs/app.mjs` (gravity and the active weapon) and, for modules in `src/`, Leaflet with the classes of
`stubs/leaflet.mjs` (empty ones, and a polygon that keeps what it was made with); `squadGrid.js`, `groundShapes.js`, `libs/leaflet-ellipse.js` and `stretchedTileLayer.js` get the real Leaflet 2 package, which `leaflet.test.mjs` imports too. `fixtures/` holds data the tests compare with: tables
read from the game files and answers of the layer API, each file naming its source.

- `squadBallistics.test.mjs`: elevation roots, range, that the solved elevation lands the shot of every kind of weapon, and that a target the muzzle is not short of has no elevation on any weapon whose muzzle is ahead of its pivot.
- `squadDamage.test.mjs`: damage falloff, cover, hit probability, rounds needed, and `damageField` with the objects of the volume layer as cover (the blocked-between-clearances callback, the landscape still covering, the impact height).
- `squadHeightmaps.test.mjs`: height interpolation (the game's triangle rule) on the grid the worker hands over (samples on the landscape's vertices: off the minimap's corner, 0.75 m apart, 1.2 m and turned), edges, the detail where objects stand (`heightmapDetail.js`: quads with and without it, the rounding of its nodes, damaged files), the loaded and failed states, height path.
- `structureDamage.test.mjs`: damage of shells to player-built structures (the native rules of ASQDeployable::TakeDamage on a made-up wall: direct-hit multiplier, no direct-radial multiplier, minimum-damage floor, only-direct near miss, exact damage-type class, burst distance; and the game data for the mortar round and HEAT against the FOB radio and a HAB, `structureOutcome`, and the links of `src/data/structureLinks.js` to the shells of `weapons.js`).
- `weapons.test.mjs`: invariants of the `weapons.js` data (among them: a Squad Ballistics name, current, of another view or from before a rename, belongs to one weapon only; the old name of a view stands for a view of the same weapon); the order of the list, frozen by `fixtures/weaponOrder.json` because the selected weapon is stored by its position (a new weapon is appended there and in `WEAPONS`); the text of every name the weapon and ammunition selectors show in each of the six `public/locales/*/weapons.json`; the elevation ranges of the artillery weapons against the pitch ranges of their mounts in the game, their minimum distances against the range at the mount's stop, and the range circles of the mortar and hell cannon markers.
- `ballisticsNames.test.mjs`: the weapon a Squad Ballistics name selects through `window.squadBallistics.selectWeapon` and the name reported back (`ballisticsNames.js`): its own name, the name of another view of it, a name from before a rename (the weapon's, reported as its current one; a view's, reported as that view), with the weapons of `weapons.js`.
- `maps.test.mjs`: the map list (25 maps, each named once and in six languages), its order, frozen by `fixtures/mapOrder.json` because a saved `.squadcalc` file and a session store the map by its position (a new map is appended there and in `MAPS`; a link names the map), the landscape origin (to the millimetre), size and scale of every map against the game's level data, and the FOB exclusion radius (`fobExclusionRadius`) of every layer of the game against `fixtures/gameFobExclusion.json` (the `FOBRadius` row of each of the 221 layers of the Squad dedicated server build 26005357; the two tutorial layers have no map here), `SMALL_FOB_LAYERS` against the 300 m layers of that table, and the radius without a layer. `squadVolume.test.mjs` also checks that a `meta.json` without a `volume` entry starts no worker and keeps the heightmap.
- `hitProbability.test.mjs`: the hit probability of direct-fire weapons from a muzzle above the ground against a simulation of the game's cone (#19).
- `volumeTerrain.test.mjs`: the `TER2` landscape file of the volume layer (decoding, a turned grid, the game's triangle rule, holes) and the `TER1` files served before it.
- `targetHeights.test.mjs`: the heights a vertical ray offers (floor, ceiling, roof, window, doorway) from the solids it crosses.
- `targetHeight.test.mjs`: a target with a height of its own in the firing solution and the line-of-fire samples.
- `structureInfo.test.mjs`: the marker near a target and the structure line of the dialog and popups.
- `targetPopup.test.mjs`: the content of a target's calculation popup (`targetPopup.js`): the line of the height chosen in the dialog, the elevation following the height, the ammunition and the weapon, the bearing line and summary as the game's compass shows the bearing, and the order of the lines.
- `groundShapes.test.mjs`: the shapes sized in metres (`groundShapes.js`) projected with Leaflet's CRS.Simple and the map's two scales: a circle of 400, 1236.9 and 2184.3 m is that radius east to west and north to south within 1 cm on each of the 25 maps (on Skorpo a Leaflet circle of R times the east scale, as drawn before, is 6.38 m too long at 2184.3 m), and a turned ellipse drawn on Skorpo, Black Coast and Al Basrah is the ellipse in metres.
- `compass.test.mjs`: the bearing as the game's HUD compass shows it (`compass.js`) against its native code (USQCompassMK2, build 26005357): three digits cut toward zero and padded, the tenth rounded within that degree, exactly north as 360, tiny negative bearings, any turn.
- `volumeLayer.test.mjs`: the volume worker end to end on a synthetic wall with a doorway and a small house (the `column` query of the target heights, the `cover` damage zone: a soldier behind the wall is covered, one in line with the doorway is not; the `heat` reverse-position heatmap: positions behind the wall blocked, those in line with the doorway clear) through the real WebAssembly engine (skipped unless `npm run build:engine` was run), and the structures of the fire-coverage classifier.
- `positionHeatmap.test.mjs`: the reverse-position heatmap's scoring (closer is better, a long shot stays viable, clearance counts), the classification of positions on flat ground, behind a ridge, out of the map and out of the weapon's reach, the polylines handed to the objects callback, the image, and the choice of a precision region.
- `squadVolume.test.mjs`: the load of the volume layer when the setting is switched while meta.json is being fetched (one worker, `volume:ready` once). `sha256.test.mjs`: the plain-JavaScript SHA-256 against `node:crypto`.
- `serversSource.test.mjs`: the servers of server mode (the home feed of the Deadline game server): the map of a layer for every map (the longest name when one map's name starts another's), a feed older than two minutes or malformed is dropped (its age counted from the `Date` of its response, from the device's clock when the response has none), a missing feed gives no servers, a read that fails leaves the servers of the last read until their feed is two minutes old (`feedReader`, `serversAfterRead`), and what server mode does with the followed server after a read of the feed (`followDecision`: open the layer it plays, stay, or leave because the server is not in the feed or plays what the calculator cannot show, `canFollow`).
- `leaflet.test.mjs`: Leaflet 2 event names (pointer, not mouse; `bubblingPointerEvents`).
- `squadGrid.test.mjs`: the map's grid (`squadGrid.js` with the real Leaflet) where the game draws it: from the game's corner of the map or of the loaded layer (`gridOffset`, `layerGridOffset`), over the game's minimap only (`gridSize`, `layerGridSize`), with as many lines of each level as the game draws, the letters along its north edge and the numbers along its west edge, the strip left after the last whole keypad labelled at its middle (Al Basrah N and 14, Fallujah K and 11 over 5 m), and Mestia's labels kept over their columns where the game's drift; against the game's labels and line counts of eight layers in `fixtures/gameKeypads.json`.
- `mapImages.test.mjs`: where the map pictures lie (`mapImages.js`, `stretchedTileLayer.js`): a layer's own minimap over the corners of the layer-minimaps manifest (Mutaha Seed v1 where the texture's edges put it, Black Coast Seed v1 over its layer data's corners), the upstream basemap over the game's unrounded corners and on Skorpo over the rectangle its texture shows on the world, the HQ tiles' grid stretched onto that rectangle on every map, and the tile level's CSS transform (a tile's pixel lands where the transform draws its map point; with the identity, as Leaflet moves it).
- `keypad.test.mjs`: the keypad text and entry (`keypad.js`, behind `squadMinimap.getKP` and `SquadCalc.getPos`) against the game's text 1 m inside the corners and at the middle of eight layers (`fixtures/gameKeypads.json`: Chora and Mutaha Skirmish v1 and Goose Bay Seed v1 with a minimap of their own, Skorpo 9.5 m and Black Coast 1 m off the map image, and Al Basrah, Fallujah and Mestia), no keypad outside the layer, the game's unpadded text, with or without its spaces, landing in the sub-keypad the game names, `formatKeyPad` while a keypad is typed, and texts that are not a keypad.
- `protectionZone.test.mjs`: what the map draws for the protection zones of a layer (`protectionShapes`, and `protectionZoneLayers`, the circles in metres and polygons `SquadLayer.createProtectionZones` puts on the map, the polygons of the stub) on the layer data of the API in `fixtures/protectionZones.json`, against the zones of the game: the capsule of Kohat TC v1 as its footprint, every box of Fallujah Invasion v2's zone of three, the point of 0.44 m of Sumari Invasion v1 and TC v1 with its no-deploy area of 50 m, none for the capsule and the zone of three; and what stays: Goose Bay AAS v1, RAAS v1 and RAAS v2, whose first main has a negative radius in the layer data, Lashkar Invasion v1, whose spheres of 0 are not drawn, Al Basrah Seed v1, whose zone of no team is not, and a turned box on Skorpo's map, scaled apart north and east. The table of the layers whose zones changed in 45.45.0 is in the test's header.

What needs a browser (hover, drag, clean console on a served build) is covered by
`tools/browser_check/browser_check.mjs` (among its checks: the exclusion circle of FOB markers through the loading, the change and the clearing of a layer, the protection circles of Goose Bay AAS v1, the capsule of Kohat TC v1 and the three boxes of Fallujah Invasion v2 on the map), `volume_check.mjs` (the volume layer), `targets_check.mjs` (target heights, structure damage), `grid_check.mjs` (the grid's place, extent and labels, the keypad text under the cursor and the entry of the game's text on Skorpo, Chora Skirmish v1 and Al Basrah) and `heatmap_check.mjs` (the position heatmap and the damage zone with voxel cover, against the exact triangle tracer's output) in the deployment repository (Playwright and Chromium).
CI: `.github/workflows/test.yml`.
